What Does Windshield Replacement Involve?

Windshield replacement starts with protective covers over the hood, fenders and dash. The technician cuts the old glass free from its urethane bond, trims the old adhesive down to a thin even layer and primes any bare metal so rust cannot start under the new seal. A fresh bead of urethane goes on, the new windshield is set and centered, and the mirror, rain sensor and moldings are put back. The install takes about an hour, plus cure time.

When Do You Need Windshield Replacement?

A crack longer than a dollar bill

Long cracks cannot be stopped with resin and keep running.

Damage in the driver’s view

Resin leaves a faint mark, so breaks right in front of the driver need new glass.

A crack at the edge

Edge cracks weaken the bond between the glass and the frame and spread fast in heat.

Pitting and glare

Years of highway sand make sunrise drives and oncoming headlights hard to see through.

A leak or a whistle

Water at the top corners or wind noise at speed points to a failed seal.

Glass caved in by debris

A falling limb or flying debris can crush a windshield in one hit.

Why Do Windshield Cracks Keep Spreading?

A windshield is two layers of glass with a clear plastic layer in between, and a stone chip breaks the outer layer. Every time the glass heats up or cools down, it expands and shrinks, and that movement pulls at the ends of the break. Florida afternoons make it worse: a windshield parked in full sun gets very hot, then the AC hits the inside with cold air. Potholes, rough unpaved roads and slammed doors do the rest.

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can I drive after a new windshield in Palm Bay?

Most urethane adhesives reach safe drive-away strength in one to three hours, depending on the product, the heat and the humidity. The technician gives you the exact time before leaving. Driving too soon can let the glass shift or weaken the bond the airbag relies on.

Can you replace a windshield when it is raining?

The glass and frame have to be dry for the adhesive to bond, so a windshield is never installed in open rain. During summer storm season we book morning appointments or work in a garage, carport or covered parking spot.

When can I wash my car after a windshield replacement?

Wait at least a day before a car wash and keep pressure washers away from the edges of the glass for a couple of days. Leaving a window open a crack on the first day also eases pressure on the fresh seal when the doors close.

Do I need ADAS calibration with my new windshield?

If there is a camera behind the rearview mirror, the answer is usually yes. The camera has to be aimed again for the new glass so lane-keep and emergency braking work the way they were designed. We confirm it for your vehicle with the estimate.
