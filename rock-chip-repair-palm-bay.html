What Does Rock Chip Repair Involve?

Chip repair starts with cleaning loose glass and dirt out of the impact point and drying any moisture trapped inside. A bridge tool attaches over the chip, pulls a vacuum to draw out the air and then pushes resin into every leg of the break. UV light cures the resin, and the surface is scraped flat and polished. A short single crack gets a tiny hole drilled at its end so it cannot keep running, then it is filled the same way.

When Do You Need Rock Chip Repair?

A fresh star or bullseye

The sooner resin goes in, the cleaner the repair looks and the better it holds.

A chip smaller than a quarter

Most chips up to about this size are good candidates for repair.

A short crack from a chip

Single cracks under about six inches can often be stopped and filled.

Two or three small chips

Several small chips can usually be repaired in the same visit.

After driving on gravel

Loose rock on unpaved and construction roads causes a lot of fresh chips.

Before a long drive

Hours at highway speed and big temperature swings are when chips spread.

Why Are Rock Chips So Common in Palm Bay?

Rock chips come from small stones flicked up by the tires of the vehicle in front of you. Palm Bay has plenty of sources: three I-95 interchanges, steady truck traffic on Malabar Road and Babcock Street, and new neighborhoods going up in the southwest part of the city. Parts of town still have unpaved limerock streets, and construction sites track gravel onto paved roads. A stone hitting glass at speed is enough to break the outer layer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will a repaired rock chip disappear completely?

A resin repair makes a chip much harder to see, but a faint mark often stays where the stone hit. The main goal is strength: the resin bonds the broken glass so it cannot spread. Chips repaired soon after they happen look the best.

Can I drive right after a chip repair?

Yes. The resin is cured with UV light before the technician leaves, so the car can be driven and washed right away. There is no adhesive cure time like there is after a full windshield replacement.

How do I keep a chip from spreading until it is fixed?

Put a piece of clear tape over it to keep out dirt and water, park in the shade and avoid pointing the defroster or AC straight at the glass. Slammed doors and hard bumps can also spread it, so book the repair soon.

Can a crack be repaired, or only chips?

A single crack shorter than about six inches that does not reach the edge of the glass can often be repaired. Longer cracks, cracks that branch in several directions and cracks at the edge usually need a new windshield.
