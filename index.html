The basics

What Is Auto Glass Repair in Palm Bay?

Auto glass repair in Palm Bay covers every piece of glass on a car, truck or SUV: filling a rock chip with resin, replacing a cracked windshield, putting in new door or back glass, and re-aiming the safety camera that looks through the windshield. Anyone who drives I-95, Malabar Road or Babcock Street every day will need some of it sooner or later.

The windshield is not just a window. It is glued to the body with urethane, it helps keep the roof from caving in during a rollover, and the passenger airbag pushes against it when it deploys. That is why the bond matters as much as the glass.

Signs to watch for

When Do You Need Windshield or Auto Glass Repair?

Most calls start with one of these moments. If one sounds familiar, the glass needs attention soon.

A stone hit on the interstate

A sharp crack, then a star or bullseye on the glass. Call while a resin repair can still save it.

Gravel and limerock roads

Unpaved streets and new construction in southwest Palm Bay throw loose rock at every car that passes.

A chip turned into a crack

Blasting the AC on a sun-baked windshield makes small chips run, especially from May through September.

A window broken overnight

A smashed door or back window from a break-in leaves the car open to rain and theft.

Pine limbs and storm debris

Tall slash pines drop limbs and cones in summer storms and hurricanes, often right onto parked cars.

A warning light after new glass

A lane-keep or collision light after a windshield job means the camera was not calibrated.

How Much Does Auto Glass Repair Cost in Palm Bay?

The cost of auto glass repair in Palm Bay depends on which glass broke and what is built into it. A rock chip repair is the simplest job. Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Auto Glass in Palm Bay

Does insurance pay for a new windshield in Palm Bay?

In most cases, yes. Florida Statute 627.7288 says drivers with comprehensive coverage do not pay a deductible for windshield damage, so a covered windshield repair or replacement usually costs nothing out of pocket. That rule applies to the windshield only. Door glass, back glass and sunroofs fall under your regular deductible.

How long does a windshield replacement take?

The install itself takes about an hour. After that, the urethane adhesive needs one to three hours before the car is safe to drive, depending on the product, the heat and the humidity. A rock chip repair is much quicker, usually about 30 minutes.

Do you come to my house or work?

Yes. Palm Bay Auto Glass Repair is a mobile service, so the technician comes to your driveway, your office parking lot or wherever the car is parked. The address on this site is a service office, not a shop where you drop the car off.

Can a rock chip be repaired instead of replacing the windshield?

Often, yes. A chip smaller than a quarter, or a single crack shorter than about six inches, can usually be filled with resin as long as it is not at the edge of the glass or right in front of the driver. Tell us the size and where it sits, and we will say which fix makes sense.

What should I do if a storm breaks my car window?

Get the car under cover if you can, keep the glass out of the seats and tape heavy plastic over the opening so rain cannot get in. Then call us. Storm-broken windshields and door glass are usually a comprehensive insurance claim, and we can help you start it.

Is it safe to drive with a cracked windshield?

A small chip outside your line of sight is not an emergency, but a long crack is a safety problem. The windshield helps hold the roof up in a rollover and gives the passenger airbag something to push against. Florida law also allows a ticket when damaged glass blocks the driver’s view.

Does my car need camera calibration after a new windshield?

If the vehicle has lane-keep assist, automatic emergency braking or adaptive cruise, the camera behind the rearview mirror usually needs to be calibrated after the glass is replaced. We check for it when we give the estimate so nothing is missed.
